{08} FAQ

Got any
Questions?

What is your typical project timeline?
Most design projects take between 2–4 weeks, depending on scope, feedback speed, and complexity. Smaller tasks can move faster.
Do you offer ongoing design support?
Yes! I offer dedicated monthly retainers and ongoing design advisory for founders and growing product teams needing steady feature iteration.
What do you need from me before starting?
A clear brief outlining your goals, target audience, any existing branding guidelines or assets, and visual references or competitors you admire.
How involved do I need to be during the project?
I respect your time. We'll have weekly async check-ins or quick Figma syncs to keep decisions moving quickly with minimal friction.
How do revisions and feedback work?
All milestones include dedicated feedback loops. Revisions are tracked directly in Figma comments to ensure full transparency and rapid turnarounds.
